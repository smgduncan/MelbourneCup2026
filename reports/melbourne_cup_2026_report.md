2. Hopewell Rock and the wet-trackers (James J Braddock, Mr Monaco, So You Are) improve if more rain falls; Point Of Law, Trustyourinstinct, Goodie Two Shoes and Zahrann prefer it drier. Re-run with `--track heavy` or `--track good` once the final rating is announced.
3. Confirm Shockletz's rider (Harry Coffey's collarbone) before backing her.
4. Use bookmaker Cup Day promotions (money back for 2nd/3rd, best tote/best fixed) on the top two picks where available.
